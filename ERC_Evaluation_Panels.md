
# ERC Evaluation Panels

ERC panels cover all fields of research in three domains: **Physical Sciences & Engineering** (`PE`), **Life Sciences** (`LS`), and **Social Sciences & Humanities** (`SH`).
The list of keywords and descriptors associated to each panel is indicative and not exhaustive;
applications are welcomed from all fields and disciplines even if not specifically mentioned under a given panel.

## Life Sciences

### `LS1` Molecular Biology, Biochemistry & Biophysics
> Molecular synthesis, modification, mechanisms and interactions, biochemistry, structural biology, molecular biophysics, signalling pathways.

1. Macromolecular complexes including interactions involving nucleic acids, proteins, lipids and carbohydrates
2. Biochemistry
3. DNA synthesis, modification, repair, recombination, degradation
4. RNA synthesis, processing, modification, degradation
5. Protein synthesis, modification, turnover
6. Lipid biology
7. Glycobiology
8. Molecular biophysics (e.g. single-molecule approaches, bioenergetics, fluorescence)
9. Structural biology and its methodologies (e.g. crystallography, cryo-EM, NMR and new technologies)
10. Molecular mechanisms of signalling pathways
11. Fundamental aspects of synthetic biology and chemical biology

### `LS2` Genetics, Omics, Bioinformatics & Systems Biology
> Molecular genetics, quantitative genetics, genetic epidemiology, epigenetics, genomics, metagenomics, transcriptomics, proteomics, metabolomics, glycomics, bioinformatics, computational biology, biostatistics, systems biology.

1. Molecular genetics, reverse genetics, forward genetics, genome editing
2. Non-coding RNAs
3. Quantitative genetics
4. Genetic epidemiology
5. Epigenetics and gene regulation
6. Genomics (e.g. comparative genomics, functional genomics)
7. Metagenomics
8. Transcriptomics
9. Proteomics
10. Metabolomics
11. Glycomics/Lipidomics
12. Bioinformatics
13. Computational biology
14. Biostatistics
15. Systems biology

### `LS3` Cellular & Developmental Biology
> Cell biology, cell physiology, signal transduction, organogenesis, developmental genetics, pattern formation and stem cell biology, in plants and animals, or, where appropriate, in microorganisms.

1. Morphology and functional imaging of cells and tissues
2. Cytoskeleton and cell behaviour (e.g. control of cell shape, cell migration and cellular mechanosensing)
3. Organelle biology and trafficking
4. Cell junctions, cell adhesion, cell communication and the extracellular matrix
5. Cell signalling and signal transduction
6. Cell cycle, division and growth
7. Cell death (including senescence) and autophagy
8. Cell differentiation, physiology and dynamics
9. Developmental genetics in animals and plants
10. Embryology and pattern formation in animals and plants
11. Tissue organisation and morphogenesis in animals and plants (including biophysical approaches)
12. Stem cell biology in development, tissue regeneration and ageing, and fundamental aspects of stem cell-based therapies

### `LS4` Physiology, Pathophysiology & Endocrinology
> Organ physiology, pathophysiology, endocrinology, metabolism, ageing, tumorigenesis, cardiovascular diseases, metabolic syndromes.

1. Organ physiology and pathophysiology
2. Comparative physiology and pathophysiology
3. Molecular aspects of endocrinology
4. Fundamental mechanisms underlying ageing
5. Metabolism, biological basis of metabolism-related disorders
6. Fundamental mechanisms underlying cancer
7. Fundamental mechanisms underlying cardiovascular diseases
8. Non-communicable diseases (except for neural/psychiatric and immunity-related diseases)

### `LS5` Neuroscience & Neural Disorders
> Neural cell function and signalling, systems neuroscience, neural bases of cognitive and behavioural processes, neurological and psychiatric disorders.

1. Neural cell function, communication and signalling, neurotransmission in neuronal and/or glial cells
2. Systems neuroscience and computational neuroscience (e.g. neural networks, neural modelling)
3. Neuronal development, plasticity and regeneration
4. Sensation and perception (e.g. sensory systems, sensory processing, pain)
5. Neural bases of cognitive processes (e.g. memory, learning, attention)
6. Neural bases of behaviour (e.g. sleep, consciousness, addiction)
7. Neurological disorders (e.g. neurodegenerative diseases, seizures)
8. Psychiatric disorders (e.g. affective and anxiety disorders, autism, psychotic disorders)
9. Neurotrauma and neurovascular conditions (including injury, blood-brain barrier, stroke, neurorehabilitation)

### `LS6` Immunity & Infection
> The immune system and related disorders, biology of infectious agents and infection, biological basis of prevention and treatment of infectious diseases.

1. Innate immunity in animals and plants
2. Adaptive immunity
3. Regulation and effector functions of the immune response (e.g. cytokines, interferons and chemokines, inflammation, immune signalling, helper T cells, immunological memory, immunological tolerance, cell-mediated cytotoxicity, complement)
4. Immunological mechanisms in disease (e.g. autoimmunity, allergy, transplantation immunology, tumour immunology)
5. Biology of pathogens (e.g. bacteria, viruses, parasites, fungi)
6. Mechanisms of infection (e.g. transmission, virulence factors, host defences, immunity to pathogens, molecular pathogenesis)
7. Biological basis of prevention and treatment of infection (e.g. infection natural cycle, reservoirs, vectors, vaccines, antimicrobials)
8. Infectious diseases in animals and plants

### `LS7` Applied Medical Technologies, Diagnostics, Therapies & Public Health
> Development of tools for diagnosis, monitoring and treatment of diseases, pharmacology, clinical medicine, regenerative medicine, epidemiology and public health.

1. Imaging for medical diagnosis
2. Genetic tools for medical diagnosis
3. Other medical technologies for diagnosis and monitoring of diseases
4. Pharmacology and pharmacogenomics (including drug discovery and design, drug delivery and therapy, toxicology)
5. Applied gene and cell therapies, regenerative medicine
6. Radiation therapy
7. Analgesia and surgery
8. Epidemiology and public health
9. Environmental health, occupational medicine
10. Health services, health care research, medical ethics

### `LS8` Ecology, Evolution & Environmental Biology
> Population, community and ecosystem ecology, evolutionary biology, behavioural ecology, microbial ecology.

1. Ecosystem and community ecology, macroecology
2. Biodiversity, conservation biology, conservation genetics
3. Population biology, population dynamics, population genetics
4. Evolutionary ecology
5. Evolutionary genetics
6. Phylogenetics, systematics, comparative biology
7. Macroevolution, palaeobiology
8. Coevolution, biological mechanisms and ecology of species interactions (e.g. symbiosis, parasitism, mutualism, food-webs)
9. Behavioural ecology and evolution
10. Microbial ecology and evolution
11. Marine biology and ecology

### `LS9` Biotechnology, Molecular & Biosystems Engineering
> Applied plant and animal sciences, forestry, food sciences, applied biotechnology, environmental and marine biotechnology, applied bioengineering, biomass and biofuels, biohazards.

1. Applied biotechnology (including transgenic organisms, applied genetics and genomics, biosensors, bioreactors, microbiology, bioactive compounds)
2. Applied bioengineering, synthetic biology, chemical biology, nanobiotechnology, metabolic engineering, protein and glyco-engineering, tissue engineering, biocatalysis, biomimetics
3. Applied animal sciences (including animal breeding, veterinary sciences, animal husbandry, animal welfare, aquaculture, fisheries, insect gene drive)
4. Applied plant sciences (including crop production, plant breeding, agroecology, forestry, soil biology)
5. Food sciences (including food technology, food safety, nutrition)
6. Biomass production and utilisation, biofuels
7. Environmental biotechnology (including bioindicators, bioremediation, biodegradation)
8. Biohazards (including biological containment, biosafety, biosecurity)
9. Marine biotechnology (including marine bioproducts, feed resources, genome mining)
